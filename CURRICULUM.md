# Study roadmap

Proposed sequence aligned with the Mathematics 1a topics discussed during planning; verify the exact current teaching order and assessment rules against the official DTU site before enrolment. The applied cases below are original extensions, not DTU assignments.

| Module | Mathematics | Applied case | Tools |
|---|---|---|---|
| 00 | Algebra, trigonometry, functions, calculus review | Solar geometry and electrical units | Python, pvlib |
| 01 | Logic | PV design constraint predicates | Python |
| 02 | Sets and functions | PV operating domain | Python, pvlib |
| 03 | Complex numbers | AC impedance and complex power | Python, SymPy |
| 04 | Polynomials | Polynomial approximations and roots | NumPy, SymPy |
| 05 | Induction | Series-connected PV string model | SymPy |
| 06 | Linear equations | Kirchhoff circuit equations | NumPy |
| 07 | Matrices and determinants | Nodal circuit analysis | NumPy, SymPy |
| 08 | Span and basis | Solar direction vectors | NumPy, pvlib |
| 09 | Vector spaces | Hourly generation profiles | NumPy, pvlib |
| 10 | Linear maps | Coordinate rotations | NumPy |
| 11 | Eigenvalues | Linear circuit dynamics | NumPy, SciPy |
| 12 | Differential equations | Simplified BESS state of charge | SciPy |
| 13 | Higher-order differential equations | RLC transient model | SciPy, SymPy |
| Exam | Mixed manual practice | Timed past papers | Per official exam rules |

## Weekly routine

45 minutes each weekday: theory, manual exercises, PV textbook, programming, applied exercise. Weekend: two hours of validation and documentation. Adapt to diagnostic results rather than advancing by calendar alone.
